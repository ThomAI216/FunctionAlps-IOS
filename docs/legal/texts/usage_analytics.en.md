## Usage statistics

Optional, and you can say no. FunctionAlps works exactly the same either way.

### What is recorded

- When you open and close the app or the members dashboard, and how long a session lasts.
- Which screens you visit.
- Whether you are on iOS, Android, the web, or the members dashboard.

### What is not recorded

- What you type.
- Your location.
- Anything you do outside FunctionAlps.

### Why

So your practitioner can see whether FunctionAlps is actually helping you, for example, noticing that you have not managed to open it for a couple of weeks and checking in, rather than assuming you stopped caring.

### Who sees it

Your practitioner and the FunctionAlps team. It is never sold and never shared with advertisers.

### Saying no

Nothing changes. You can turn this on or off whenever you like in Privacy & Data, in the app or on the members dashboard.
