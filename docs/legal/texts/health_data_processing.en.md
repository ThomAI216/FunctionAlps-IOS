## Processing of your health data across FunctionAlps

Health data is sensitive personal data. Swiss and EU law require your explicit, separate and freely given permission before it may be processed at all — which is why this is asked on its own, in plain words, and not folded into the Terms. Please read it rather than skim it: it is the consent the entire service rests on.

### 1. What counts as your health data here

Nearly everything FunctionAlps holds about you falls into this category — and it is one record, whether you entered it in the app, on the members dashboard, or in an assessment on our website. Specifically:

- Meals — the photographs you take, your written or spoken descriptions, the foods and portions identified, and the nutritional and micronutrient estimates derived from them.
- Daily check-ins — sleep, energy, mood and stress, your bed and wake times, and any free text you write.
- Digestive and symptom entries — bloating, stool quality, related symptoms, and any safety flags you report.
- Supplements you tell us you take, and measurements you record such as weight, height and body-composition estimates.
- Profile facts that reveal health — age, sex, health goals and current complaints.
- Everything we derive from the above: scores, patterns, observations, reports, and the summary of a website assessment.

### 2. What you are agreeing to

That FunctionAlps may collect, store, organise and analyse that information for one purpose: providing you the service — showing you what you logged, deriving your scores and patterns, producing your reports, and enabling your practitioner and the FunctionAlps care team to support you.

This one permission covers the whole of FunctionAlps: the app, the members dashboard, the assessments on our website, and the emails that deliver your results. We ask you once. You will not be asked again on a different screen for the same processing, because it is the same processing and the same operator — and being asked twice for one thing would make it less clear what you had agreed to, not more.

The analysis is carried out on our own infrastructure, by our AI provider in Switzerland and, to identify the foods in your meals, by OpenAI in the United States, as described in the Privacy Notice and in section 3 below.

### 3. AI is part of this

FunctionAlps uses AI for defined parts of the service: identifying foods in a photograph or description, estimating portions, converting voice to text, scoring meals, detecting patterns, drafting your reports, and writing the summary of a website assessment. Agreeing here covers that processing — it is not a separate decision, because there is no version of the service that works without it.

Identifying the foods in a meal photograph or description runs on OpenAI in the United States; the rest of the analysis runs on Infomaniak’s infrastructure in Geneva, Switzerland. Apart from those meal photographs and descriptions, your data is not sent to OpenAI, Google, Anthropic or any other general-purpose AI provider, and it is never used to train anyone’s models.

We remove your name, email address and contact details before anything is sent for analysis. We do not claim the result is anonymous: a photograph can show a face or a room, and free text or a voice recording can carry a name. It stays your personal data, with every protection and right attached.

The separate note "How AI is used" sets out what the AI does well, where it gets things wrong, and what is checked by a person before you see it. Please read it — it is shown alongside this consent.

### 4. What this consent does not cover

This permission is limited to providing your service. It does not authorise any of the following, each of which is a separate choice you can decline with no effect whatsoever on your service:

- sharing your health data with anyone outside FunctionAlps and its named service providers;
- using it for marketing, or to decide what marketing you see;
- using it for research, even anonymised research;
- using it to train AI models, ours or anyone else’s;
- selling it, or disclosing it to advertisers, insurers or employers — which we do not do under any circumstances, with or without consent.

### 5. Why it is required rather than optional

Processing your health data is not a feature of FunctionAlps that sits alongside others — it is what FunctionAlps is. There is no version of it that does something useful without it.

We ask before you start, rather than burying it in a setting, precisely because it is unavoidable: you should be able to decide with the consequence in front of you, and walk away at no cost if the answer is no.

We are aware that a consent you cannot refuse without losing the service is a consent under pressure. That is why the optional items are genuinely optional, why withdrawal is always available, and why declining here costs you nothing beyond not having the service — no charge, no account, no data kept.

### 6. Who can see your health data

You — in the app and on the members dashboard, which show you the same record. Your practitioner and the FunctionAlps care team, so they can support you. The small number of people who administer the service, where they need access to operate or secure it, under confidentiality obligations.

Our service providers process it strictly on our instructions and may never use it for their own purposes; they are named in the Privacy Policy, together with where they operate.

Where a pattern or a flagged symptom may deserve attention, a summary may be reviewed, and your identity is re-associated only where a reviewer needs to follow up with you. Every such look-up is recorded in an internal audit log.

### 7. How long it is kept

While your account is active, so your own history and trends stay available to you. If you delete your account, your health data is erased within 30 days.

Two things survive: records your practitioner is legally required to keep, which are retained by them separately from FunctionAlps for the period Swiss law requires; and the record of this consent itself, which contains no health data and exists to prove we processed yours lawfully.

### 8. Withdrawing

You can withdraw at any time in Privacy & Data — in the app or on the members dashboard, whichever you have to hand. Withdrawal is always available, takes effect immediately, is deliberately no harder than giving it was, and does not affect the lawfulness of anything we did while the consent was in force.

Because the service consists of this processing, withdrawing switches FunctionAlps off — the app and the members dashboard together, since it was one consent for one service. That is a consequence, not a penalty: there is simply nothing we can do for you without it.

Withdrawing does not delete your data. You can give consent again and pick up where you left off, or delete your account to erase everything. If you want a copy first, export your data before you do either.

### 9. Your rights

You can see, correct, export or delete your health data at any time, restrict or object to processing, and complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner or, in the EEA, your local data protection authority. The Privacy Policy explains each right, how to use it, and the deadlines we work to.
