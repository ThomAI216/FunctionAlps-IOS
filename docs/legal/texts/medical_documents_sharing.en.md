## Sharing your medical documents with your care team

This consent is separate from the one you gave when you joined. It covers only what you add in Lab Results on the members dashboard: Previous records and Your medical history.

### 1. What this covers

The documents you add on the members dashboard (past lab reports, PDFs, screenshots, photos of paper results, letters and reports from other health professionals, discharge summaries, imaging reports) and the written or voice notes you attach to them or send alongside them, including the account of your medical history that you write or record in Your medical history.

### 2. What you agree to

That the FunctionAlps care team following you may open, read, listen to and download these documents and notes, keep them in your patient file, and use them to prepare and deliver your care within FunctionAlps.

Nothing else. They are not shared with anyone outside your care team, not used for marketing, and never used to train any artificial-intelligence model, ours or anyone else's.

### 3. How they are kept

Encrypted in transit and at rest, on servers in the European Union, under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection and, where it applies to you, the GDPR. Only the practitioners following you can open them, and every view and download is logged. The Privacy Notice lists our providers and where each one operates.

### 4. Deleting a file or a note

You can delete any file or note from your space at any time, and it is removed at once. If your care team has already saved it to your clinical record, that copy is kept for the period Swiss law requires practitioners to keep medical records, and is then destroyed.

### 5. Withdrawing this consent

You can withdraw it at any time in Profile › Consents or in Lab Results. New uploads and notes stop and your files and notes are removed from your space; copies already saved to your clinical record are kept as described above. Withdrawing does not affect what was done before.

### 6. It is your choice

Sharing past documents and your medical history here is optional. Your care continues the same if you do not; you can always bring them to a consultation instead.
