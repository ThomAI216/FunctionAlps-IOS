## News and offers

Optional. If you agree, we may email you occasionally about new programmes, articles and offers. This is the only marketing we send, from anywhere in FunctionAlps.

### What to expect

- Never more than roughly once a month.
- Nothing to do with the service messages we must send you about your account or these documents, or with the emails that deliver a result you asked for — those are not marketing and are not covered by this choice.
- Every email has an unsubscribe link.
- Your health data is never used to target these, and we never share your address with anyone else.

### Saying no

Declining has no effect whatsoever on your service. You can change your mind at any time in Privacy & Data, in the app or on the members dashboard, and every email carries an unsubscribe link.
