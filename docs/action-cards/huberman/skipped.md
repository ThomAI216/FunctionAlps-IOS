# g3 — skipped protocols

| huberman-protocols:P064 | Exercise hydration (Galpin Equation) (huberman:P064) | A formula (body weight in lb ÷ 30 = oz every 15–20 min) in US units, plus +50–100 % for heavy sweaters, sauna volumes and electrolyte products or added salt. - P068: dropped the calorie-percentage targets (fat 30–35 %, saturated ≤ 10 %, trans ≤ 1 %).
