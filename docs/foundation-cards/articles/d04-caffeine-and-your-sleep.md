Caffeine stays in your body for hours, so your last coffee or tea of the day should come before 16:00.

Many people say, "Coffee doesn't affect my sleep, I fall asleep fine." Sometimes that is true. But caffeine can still make sleep lighter and shorter without you noticing. Then you wake up tired, reach for another coffee, and the loop starts again.

## How caffeine works

Through the day, a substance called adenosine builds up in your brain. The more of it there is, the sleepier you feel. This "sleep pressure" is part of what helps you fall asleep at night. Caffeine works by blocking adenosine's effect. You still build up sleep pressure; you just feel it less.

The catch is that caffeine leaves the body slowly. It takes many hours to clear, and how fast varies a lot from person to person: genes, age, hormones, smoking and some medicines all play a part. So an afternoon coffee can still be working at bedtime.

## What the research shows

In a well-known trial, a strong dose of caffeine taken six hours before bed still disturbed people's sleep. A 2023 review that pooled 24 studies found caffeine shortened total sleep time and reduced deep sleep, the most restoring stage. From these results, the authors estimate that a usual cup of coffee should come almost nine hours before bed to avoid losing sleep.

16:00 is a practical limit that works for most days. If you go to bed early, or you know you are sensitive to caffeine, earlier is better.

## What counts, and what to drink instead

Caffeine is in more than coffee:

- Coffee, including espresso and iced coffee.
- Black and green tea, including matcha and iced tea.
- Cola and energy drinks.
- Maté, which is often mistaken for a herbal tea.
- Cocoa and dark chocolate have smaller amounts.

After 16:00, choose decaf, a real herbal tea (such as camomile, verbena, peppermint or rooibos) or water.

## What to do

The habit is **No caffeine after 16:00**.

- Coffee, black and green tea, cola and energy drinks all count.
- After 16:00: decaf, herbal tea or water.
- If 16:00 is hard, move it 30 minutes earlier each day.

If you drink a lot of caffeine, cutting back fast can bring a headache or tiredness for a few days. Going step by step makes it easier.

## What to look for

Many people notice:

- Falling asleep faster.
- Deeper sleep.
- Needing less caffeine the next morning.

**Enjoy your coffee in the morning, and let 16:00 be your last cup.**
