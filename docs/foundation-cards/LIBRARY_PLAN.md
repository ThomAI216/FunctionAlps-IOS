# The library · plan (Thomas, 2026-11-07)

The library is being reworked alongside the Foundation Track. Mockup: the "The library" section of the Foundation Track
mockup (artifact `X4SrfHtE3Pn7UYuEvf4rjj`). This page keeps the decisions and the work still to do.

## Decisions

- **On the Foundation Track, the whole library is open.** A lead on the track reads every article, including the
  members-only ones (`patient_tier_1`), for as long as the track runs. Every article the track links is visible in the library.
- **The track's days are soft-locked, never hard-locked.** A read from a day still to come shows its title; tapping it
  says when it opens ("Opens on day 11"). It cannot be opened before its day.
- **English only for now.** The French articles (the 26 free `public_resources`) are hidden from the app.
- **No show for now.** The show leaves the Library tab.
- **The tracks live inside the pillars.** Build Your Engine and Build Your Stamina (today `energie`) and Build Muscle &
  Functional Capacity go under Movement; Fuel the Adaptation under Nutrition.
- **Supplements get their own page**, with a short selection and an article for each.
- **Titles only**, no striped cover backgrounds. Videos play in the app: our own player for our videos, YouTube's player
  for YouTube's.

## Search and ask (Thomas, 2026-11-07)

- Search by topic or keyword: the topics a word belongs to, then articles, habits and videos.
- Ask a question: the best article first, with the passage that answers it; then what is related.
- Nothing fits: "Do you want us to write it for you?" The request reaches the practice (it can feed STUDIO's article
  requests, `library_creation_requests`, as a member request). When it is written: an email to the member, and the article
  appears at the top of their library ("Written for you").
- In the reader, "Practice it" lists the habits an article goes with: open it if it is in your actions, "+ Add to my
  actions" if not.
- Videos: at the top of the page; YouTube's player inside the app, which can grow; turning the phone plays full screen.
- Supplements: on the side for now; a separate conversation builds the supplement research agent and the supplement library.

## The four tracks · review (2026-11-07)

**What they are.** Not four tracks: one training programme in four phases (the kickers say "Phase 1 · Article 1" to
"Phase 4 · Article 4"): build your engine, then stamina, then muscle and capacity, then fuel. Good material, written for
someone who already trains and follows a programme: "your current 3–4 full-body sessions", "initial experiment", a
green-yellow-red dose check, "this programme", "in this phase" (8 of the 15 lessons). All 15 are members-only.

| Track | Lessons (words) | What works | What to change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Build Your Engine | Build Your Engine (940) · Signals Create Adaptation | The five systems of your engine; too small, right, too large a dose | Signals Create Adaptation also belongs in the members' story (chapter 5); a track of 2 is thin |
| Build Your Stamina | Build Your Aerobic Base (825) · Raise Your Aerobic Ceiling (619) · HIIT: Work, Recover, Repeat (626) | A clear base → ceiling → intervals path; post-meal walking inside | Older twins in the library: Zone 2 Cardio, Zones 4 & 5, HIIT |
| Build Muscle & Functional Capacity | Build a Stronger, More Capable Body (315) · Make Your Training Work Better (437) · Exercise Snacks (374) · Strength Is More Than Muscle Size (573) | Capacity beyond muscle; eccentric control; snacks | "Make Your Training Work Better" is written for one person's 3–4 sessions; the opener is short; twins: Micro-Workouts / Exercise Snacks, Eccentric Training, Nervous System & Muscle Recruitment |
| Fuel the Adaptation | Overview (235) · Fuel & Cellular Machinery (491) · Refuel the Training Signal (561) · Micronutrient Tracking (451) · Nutrient-Dense Foods (240) · Micronutrients & Hormones (1,116) | Food first; tracking to learn, not obsess | Training-centred; the plate overlaps day 8 (Build your plate); Micronutrients & Hormones is the long bridge to supplements |

**Proposed structure, by pillar**

- Start here · *Functional health, from the ground up*: the members' story (10 chapters, above).
- Nutrition · *Fuel your body* (from Fuel the Adaptation): Build your plate (day 8) → Fuel & Cellular Machinery →
  Nutrient-Dense Foods → Micronutrient Tracking → Micronutrients & Hormones; Refuel the Training Signal as "if you train".
- Movement · *Build your engine* (Engine + Stamina merged, 5 lessons): Build Your Engine → Aerobic Base → Aerobic Ceiling → HIIT;
  Signals Create Adaptation moves to the members' story.
- Movement · *Build a stronger body* (4 lessons + the twins folded in as "go deeper").
- Sleep · *Sleep deeper* (new): Light sets your body clock · Caffeine and your sleep · The 3-2-1 rule · Your evening routine ·
  NSDR, explained · Morning Wake-Up Routine · Biological Recovery.
- Mental and emotional health · *Calm and recover* (new): Breathe: the time you give yourself · Three good things ·
  Connection is health · The habit of writing · Breathing Exercises · Recovery in Three Parts.

**Edits for all 15**: drop the "Phase X · Article Y" kickers (number within the track); replace "the programme / this
phase" with neutral wording; rewrite the one-person passages for any reader; add the "Practice it" habits (e.g. Aerobic
Base → a short walk after every meal; Exercise Snacks → 2 to 3 movement moments a day; Fuel → protein at every meal);
retire or fold in the twins.
