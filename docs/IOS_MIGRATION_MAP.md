## Supplements (2026-11-12, supplements programme; CM OS migration 333)

The practice's supplements on the phone: **Home → "Supplements today"** (under the meal and check-in squares), **Plan → "Supplements"** (observance written first), **a supplement's page** (`Route.supplement(planId)`, deep link `functionalps://supplement/<planId>`; `functionalps://supplements` = Home), and **reminders**. Backend (JWT-only RPCs, the phone formats only): `member_supplements()` → rows (`id` = the supplement plan id, `moments[]` morning · noon · evening · bedtime · day, `days_rule`, `due_today`, `today[]` ticks, `observance {days, due, taken, pct, band, strip[]}`, `client_share`, brand/product link…) and `member_log_supplement(p_plan, p_moment, p_taken, p_day)` (idempotent; today or yesterday only; `42501` not a member, `P0002` not found, `22023` bad moment/day). Layers: `FunctionAlpsBackend.supplements()` / `logSupplement` (defaults: none / no-op, so test stubs and the showcase compile) → `SupabaseBackend` → `SupplementService` (shared, observable; optimistic tick, put back on failure) → `SupplementPageViewModel` → views in `Features/Supplements/` + `Features/Home/HomeSupplementsCard`. Pure logic, tested (`Tests/Core/SupplementTests.swift`): `SupplementLogic` (current moment — morning until 11:00, midday until 15:00, evening until 21:00, then night; Home rows; written adherence "11 of 14 days" / "4 of 7 due"; strip marks), `SupplementPageBlocks` (what a page shows), `SupplementReminders` (the grouped plan).

**Page** (mockup `R7_iOSSupplementPageV2`, owner's changes): supplement + why · **What it does in your body (2nd card)** · adherence with the 14-day strip and today's switch(es) · how to take it · your brand · prescribed by · reminder row (→ Notifications). **No "What the studies say" card.** Blocks the RPC does not return yet — what it does in your body (`what_it_does`, `body_areas`), composition, price, pack, product image, learn more — are optional fields on `Supplement` and are simply absent until the server sends them (and only when `client_share` allows). Only `https` links leave the app.

**Reminders** (`S4_iOSReminders`): ON by default (`patient_notification_preferences.supplement_reminders_enabled`, `supplement_times` jsonb morning 08:00 · noon 12:30 · evening 19:00 · bedtime 22:00; Settings → Notifications → **Supplements**: master switch + four times). No opt-in sheet: the first time supplements are shown on Home the phone's notification permission is explained once (alert) and then asked. One **grouped** local notification per moment with something due, "Evening: Iberogast, Magnesium" (single → its dose, several → "N supplements to take."), only on days something is due, never for a moment already ticked, quiet hours respected (`TrackLogic.quietAdjusted`: morning/midday wait for the window's end, evening/night come 15 min before it). Category `FA_SUPPLEMENT`: **Taken** logs every supplement of the group through `SupplementService` without opening the app (also from a cold start: replayed once the service exists), **Later** asks again in 30 minutes (`fa.supp.later.<moment>.<day>`, left alone by `apply`). Re-planned whenever supplements load, a tick lands, preferences change or the app comes forward. Request ids carry the time and a checksum of the supplement ids, so a change replaces the pending request. What the phone can know: today exactly, and the next two days for a **daily** supplement; alternate-day and set-day supplements join the plan on the day they are due (every foreground re-plans). "Any time" supplements have no moment, so no reminder. Not yet: logging yesterday from the UI (the RPC allows it), a French text (owner: English only for now — `Localizable.xcstrings` entries are `en`), showcase data for these screens, the weekday names of `days_of_week` (convention unconfirmed), supplements inside the older `CarePlanView` fallback (the Plan card is on `ClientPlanView`).
